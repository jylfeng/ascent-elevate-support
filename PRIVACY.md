# Privacy Policy for Ascent

**Effective Date:** December 18, 2025

## 1. Introduction
Ascent ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application (the "App") collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect the following types of information to provide our AI coaching services:

### A. Personal Information
- **Account Information:** When you sign up, we collect your email address and a user ID to manage your account.
- **User Content:** We collect videos and photos you upload to analyze your athletic performance.

### B. Usage & Device Information
- **Device Identifiers:** We may collect device IDs for app functionality and to display relevant advertisements via Google AdMob.
- **Usage Data:** We track product interaction (e.g., how often you analyze a video) to improve app stability and performance.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
- **AI Analysis:** Your uploaded videos are processed by our backend and third-party AI providers (Google Gemini) solely for the purpose of generating coaching feedback.
- **Authentication:** We use Supabase to securely manage your login credentials.
- **Advertising:** We use Google AdMob to display rewarded advertisements to free users.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We share data with the following trusted third-party service providers to operate the App:
- **Supabase:** For database hosting and authentication.
- **Google Gemini (AI):** For video processing and analysis.
- **Google AdMob:** For displaying advertisements.
- **RevenueCat:** For processing subscription payments.

## 5. Data Retention
We retain your uploaded content and account information only as long as necessary to provide the service. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting support.

## 6. Children’s Privacy
Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
